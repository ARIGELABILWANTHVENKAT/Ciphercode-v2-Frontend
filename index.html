Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's exactly how we handle your data.

01 Information We Collect

When you use CipherCode, we collect only what's necessary to provide our services:

  • Account data: Your chosen nickname and a unique ID you create.
  • Pattern credential: Your 4-symbol pattern is salted & hashed (PBKDF2) locally — it never leaves your device.
  • Usage data: Anonymous analytics about which features you use.
  • Local storage: Code drafts, chat history, preferences stored in your browser.
  • AI conversations: Prompts are transmitted to Google's Gemini API for processing.

02 How We Use Your Data

  • To personalize your learning experience and remember your progress.
  • To power the AI Tutor with contextual, helpful responses.
  • To improve our curriculum and detect technical issues.
  • We never sell your data to third parties.

03 Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini API — powers the AI Tutor responses.
  • Judge0 CE — remote code execution for compiled languages.
  • Pyodide — runs Python entirely in your browser.

04 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security including encrypted transmissions (HTTPS), hashed session tokens, and local-only storage of sensitive data.

05 Your Rights

  • Access: View all data we have on you at any time.
  • Deletion: Clear your session and local data anytime.
  • Opt-out: Use CipherCode without creating an account.

06 Children's Privacy

CipherCode is designed for learners aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

07 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be announced on the homepage.

🛡️

TL;DR: We collect minimal data, never sell it, and store most things locally in your browser. Your code is your code.
